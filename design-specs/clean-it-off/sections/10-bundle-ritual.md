### Gotchas

- **Background is cropped in Figma; the export is not.** The image fill is STRETCH with an `imageTransform` (scale 0.6143 × 0.5926, offset 0.0000792 / 0.2127). Only source pixels **x 0–2516, y 368–1392** (of 4096 × 1728) are visible, zoomed about 1.63× and showing the left ~61% of the photo.
  - With the full PNG and `object-fit: cover`, the products appear much smaller and the hotspots will not line up.
  - **Pre-crop the asset to 2516 × 1024** before uploading, or use `background-size: 162.8% auto; background-position: 0% 52.2%`.
- **Fill order**: the white solid fill is **under** the image. It only shows while the image loads, so `#28201F` is a better placeholder. That is the photo's own backdrop colour, sampled from the PNG.
- **Mixed-style heading**: the characters are `"Five products. \none complete ritual."`, with a **trailing space before the hard line break**. Blauer Nue 500 covers "Five products. ". Tiempos 400 italic covers "one" and "complete ritual.". The **space between "one" and "complete" is Blauer Nue 500 (roman)**, an override quirk; render the whole second line in `<em>`.
  - Line-heights differ per run (69.58 vs 70.3). The node is 140 tall (2 × 70).
- **textCase UPPER**: the product title source is "CLEAN IT OFF\nFace Wash 100ml" (mixed case) and renders as "FACE WASH 100ML". The hotspot digits also carry UPPER, which has no visible effect. Use `text-transform: uppercase`.
- **Montserrat 700**: the theme accent font is `montserrat_n5` (500). Load the bold face, e.g. `{{ settings.font_accent_family | font_modify: 'weight', 'bold' | font_face }}`, or Montserrat 700 renders as faux-bold.
