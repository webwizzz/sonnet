### Gotchas

- **Before and after are the same raster** (`19939496…png`, a 736 × 1104 marketing composite with its own "BEFORE & AFTER / 4 WEEKS" text). Figma shows two zoomed crops via `imageTransform` (scaleMode STRETCH):
  - Before shows x 0.44%–49.6% and y 33.8%–60.8% of the image (≈362 × 298 source px stretched to 851 × 700, about 2.35× upscaled, so it's blurry).
  - After shows x 75.2%–99.3% and y 34.1%–60.6% (≈178 × 293 px into 425 × 700).
  - **Don't ship this file.** Ask for two separate, high-res, identically framed before/after photos and upload them through the two `image_picker`s.
- In Figma, the "after" layer is only drawn over the right half (425 px, x 1396 → 1821) rather than being a full-size image that gets clipped. For a working slider, render **both images at full panel size** and clip the after image.
- **Width mismatch**: the card is 850 px and the panel is **851 px**, so the group is 1721 px (1 px wider than the 1720 container). Use equal `1fr 1fr` columns.
- **textCase UPPER on mixed-case source**: `Ananya R` renders `ANANYA R`. `SENSITIVE SKIN` and `MY SKIN STOPPED FEELING TIGHT.` also carry textCase UPPER (their source strings are already uppercase). Store sentence-case text in the settings and apply `text-transform: uppercase` in CSS.
- **Mixed-style heading**: the second run switches to Test Tiempos Fine **400 italic** with its own line-height (70.30 vs 69.58). Use `'Test Tiempos Fine Italic RG'` (not `'Test Tiempos Fine Italic'`, which is 500).
- **Montserrat** is used only on the tag pill. Use the theme accent font (`settings.font_accent_family`), not Blauer Nue.
- The rating text is **pure black `#000000`**, not the brand `#28201F`.
- **4.5 rating = 4 filled + 1 outline star.** There's no half star in the design. Decide whether to round or draw half stars, and keep the outline star stroke at 0.8 px.
- **Arrow buttons are 46 px** (the theme `.arrow-slider` is 48 px) and only **2 px** apart.
- The handle's **radius 50 is larger than half its width** (38/2 = 19). CSS `border-radius: 50px` gives the same capsule, so this is fine.
- Line 11 and the chevron vectors are **rotated** in Figma (`rotation` 1.57 / 0.84 / 2.30 rad). Use the SVG export rather than rebuilding them from the node geometry.
- The body text box has a **fixed 637 px width** (it wraps to exactly 3 lines at 24px). If the width changes, the line breaks change too.
- The divider uses 10% opacity (`#28201F1A`), while the header divider uses 9%.
