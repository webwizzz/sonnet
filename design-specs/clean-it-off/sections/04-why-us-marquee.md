### Gotchas

- **The inner inset is 150 px**, not the 100 px `.main-container`. The label starts at x=150 and the viewport ends at x=1770.
- **`Rectangle 10` has a hidden stroke**: `visible:false`, `#28201F` @ 7%, top and bottom 1 px only (`individualStrokeWeights`). It doesn't render in Figma. Don't add borders.
- **Lines are rotated** (`rotation=1.57` rad = 90°) with stroke align OUTSIDE. In CSS they are simply 1 px wide vertical bars of 34 px (label) or 20 px (items).
- **`Rectangle 12` is rotated 180°** (`rotation=3.14`). Its fill gradient is the same as `Rectangle 11`, so the rotation is what mirrors it. Use `to left` in CSS.
- The track is wider than the viewport (1824 > 1770), so `All Skin Types` is cut mid-word and faded. That's the marquee snapshot, not a layout to reproduce.
- The No Parabens → All Skin Types divider is at 31 px (rounding). Use 30 px everywhere.
- Glyph stroke weights differ (1.25 / 0.9 / 0.3 + fill). The SVG exports already handle this, so don't restroke them in CSS.
- The Made In India SVG uses `<mask>` / `clip-path` ids, and so do the All Skin Types and Postbiotic SVGs. When the track is duplicated for the loop, the duplicate ids can break rendering in some browsers. Prefer `<img src>` for the icons, or uniquify the ids.
- The existing `marquee-bar.liquid` uses `#F5EEE6` fades, a 9 px icon gap and 28 px padding. Those are **not** these values (this design uses `#EAE7E2`, 12 px and 30 px).
