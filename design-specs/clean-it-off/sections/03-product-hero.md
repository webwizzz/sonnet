### Gotchas

- **Coordinate base:** node y values are relative to page y=**132** (`frameOffsetY`), not 112. The README's "Top y 112" is the screenshot crop, which includes the 20 px top padding.
- **Background:** the beige section background is the ignored `Rectangle 1139962724` (`#F5EEE6`, page y112–1826). Without it, the white cards and the `#FFFFFFCC` accordions sit on the near-white `#FFFCF9` page.
- **Not on `.main-container`:** the section has a 20 px left margin and a 170 px right margin, so it is visually off-center.
- **Info column offset:** it starts 30 px lower than the gallery (title at y30).
- **Rows wider than the 670 column:**
  - Badge pills: 685 wide (to x1765).
  - Video row: 690 wide (to x1770), because card 3 is **230** wide, not 220.
- **Rows narrower than 670:** pack-size row 589, upsell row 616. The description box is 635.
- **Play buttons:** on video cards 1 and 2 they are **5 px right of center** (circle center x1195 vs card center 1190; x1425 vs 1420). They were probably copied from the 230 px card. Center them in the build.
- **Card 3 styling differs:** overlay at 0% (vs 10%) and its caption box lacks the `#FFFFFF1A` layer. Use one style for all three.
- **Gallery image 1 has stacked fills:** 191:60 has 4 fills: `#FEEFEE`, two STRETCH images (a4bbc939…, 399c342a… at 11 MB), then the top FILL image ab36657f…. Only ab36657f is visible.
- **Hidden effects:** the DROP_SHADOW effects on "image 41" and "Sonet wellness serum 3" are all `visible:false` (up to 5 stacked shadows). Do not add shadows. Icon frames also carry hidden SOLID fills; ignore them.
- **Misleading layer names:** "Sonet wellness serum 3" is the **face-wash tube** image, and "image 41" is the **serum** bottle.
- **Placeholder data:**
  - All pack prices are "Rs. 1,199" and the compare-at is also "Rs. 1,199", with a "10% OFF" badge, so the math doesn't add up.
  - The badge text has a trailing space: `"10% OFF "`.
  - Pack card 1 shows only the serum, not the face wash.
  - Both upsell cards are titled "RUB IT IN SERUM", but card 2 shows the tube.
  - The product card's two pills are both "100% VEGAN".
- **Uppercase transforms:**
  - `textCase: UPPER` on lowercase source text: "Priya M." renders as PRIYA M.; "Dermatologist Tested" and "Cruelty Free" in the badge pills; also on the title, accordion titles and upsell titles.
  - Badge "100% VEGAN" has no textCase. Apply `text-transform: uppercase` to the whole pill class.
  - The ATC price source is "RS. 1,199", while every other price is "Rs.". Uppercase the ATC price with CSS.
- **Letter-spacing exceptions:** it is **0** (Figma "None") on benefit items, trust items and ATC texts. Everything else is -0.02em.
- **Line-height exception:** badge pills use a **fixed 16px** line-height (PIXELS, 123.08%), not a percentage.
- **Hard line break in the review quote:** "…for my⏎acne-prone…". Use a textarea with `newline_to_br`.
- **Rating mismatch:** the headline stars are 4 full plus 1 outline (no half-star), while the review card shows "4.5" with a single star. Stars use `#F69104`; the review star uses `#28201F`.
- **Icon stroke colors are inconsistent:** truck `#28201F`, lock and return `#000000`. Use `currentColor` = `#28201F`. The return-icon vectors have `rotation=3.14` (180°); use the exported SVG as is.
- **Dividers are rotated lines:** the vertical dividers are LINE nodes with `rotation=1.57` (w0×h18 / h14). Build them as 1px pseudo-elements. Benefits/trust dividers are 8% opacity, the review divider is 10%.
- **Full-pill radii:** the large radii are just pills. Accordion radius 90 on h62, ATC radius 44 on h52, badges and "+ADD" radius 40, outline and badge pills radius 30. `border-radius: 999px` works for all of them.
- **Bars have no radius:** the benefits and trust bars have top and bottom borders only (individual strokes 1/0/1/0) and no radius.
- **Image cropping:** the "image 41" (serum) fills use STRETCH with an `imageTransform` scale of 1.896 horizontally, which crops the side margins of the 661×1741 PNG. Video thumbs also use a crop transform. Use `object-fit: cover` (or `contain` for the cutouts) instead of reproducing the transforms.
- **Contact-shadow ellipses** use a radial gradient that ends in **white**, not transparent. On the beige background, use `rgba(255,255,255,0)` or `transparent` as the end stop.
